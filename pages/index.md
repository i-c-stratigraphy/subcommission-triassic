## Subcommission on Triassic Stratigraphy

The Subcommission on Triassic Stratigraphy is a constituent body of the
[International Commission on Stratigraphy (ICS)](https://stratigraphy.org/), within the
[International Union of Geological Sciences (IUGS)](https://www.iugs.org/).

The Subcommission coordinates international work on Triassic stratigraphy and contributes to the ICS goal of
establishing a standard global stratigraphical scale.

See the [Executive]({{ '/executive' | relative_url }}) for officers and contact information, and the
[International Chronostratigraphic Chart](https://stratigraphy.org/chart) for the global geological time scale.
