## Announcement: International Summer School on Geology, Stratigraphy, Paleontology, Historical Geology of the non-Marine Triassic of Madygen Geopark, Kyrgyzstan

**Dates:** August 14 – 26, 2022

**Location**: Southern Tian-Shan (Batken Region), southwestern Kyrgyzstan

**Website**: <https://geotianshan.org/en/expeditions/international-summer-school/>

**Organizers/instructors**: Professors and scientists from Germany, Kyrgyzstan, Kazakhstan and Uzbekistan are invited as
field mentors and trainers of the Summer School.

**Course Information:** We invite young professionals, students and scientists to take part in the Summer School at the
Madygen Geopark in the Southern Tian-Shan in Kyrgyzstan on the geology, stratigraphy, paleontology, within the Madygen
Formation, an exceptional non-marine fossil lagerstätte of the mid-Triassic. Upon completion, all participants will
receive certificates from the organizers: the UNESCO Cluster Office in Almaty, from the International Commission
Stratigraphic.

Information on the Magdygen Formation can be found
[here]({{ '/assets/albertiana/2022/01/Madygen-chart-UNESCO-p1.pdf' | relative_url }}) .

**For more information, contact:**  Dr. Alexey Dudashvili, <info@geotianshan.org>
