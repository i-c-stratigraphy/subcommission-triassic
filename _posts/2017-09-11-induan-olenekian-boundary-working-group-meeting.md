## Induan-Olenekian Boundary Working Group Meeting

**Date**: November 2-4, 2017

**Location**: Universität Innsbruck, Institut für Geologie und Paläontologie  
Innrain 52, 6020 Innsbruck, Austria

**Organizers**: Micha Horasek, Evelyn Kustatscher, and Rainer Brandner.

For Additional Information Contact: Charles Henderson at <cmhender@ucalgary.ca> and Micha Horacek at
<micha.horacek@josephinum.at>

**Meeting Information**: The point and section for the GSSP of the Induan-Olenekian Boundary (Lower Triassic) has been
debated for many years (e.g. see Albertiana volumes 29-37, 40 among many other papers). A new working group chaired by
Charles Henderson is working toward resolving this long debate. To this end, members of the IOBWG have been invited to
discuss the merits of proposed IOB GSSPs, particularly in light of recent new research – this will be the only topic to
be discussed. Other STS members interested to attend or to provide opinions should contact Charles Henderson and Micha
Horacek.
