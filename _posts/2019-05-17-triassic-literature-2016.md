## Triassic literature – 2016

[Warrington, G. 2019. Triassic Literature – 2016. Albertiana, vol. 45, 5–38.]({{ '/assets/albertiana/2019/05/45-2-Warrington.pdf' | relative_url }})

This compilation is based on the contents of over 500 serial titles and other publications. It is a continuation of the
New Triassic Literature contributions that appeared in
[![45-2-Cover]({{ '/assets/albertiana/2019/05/45-2-Cover.jpg' | relative_url }}) ]({{
'/assets/albertiana/2019/05/45-2-Warrington.pdf' | relative_url }})*Albertiana* up to December 2017 (44: 33–48), and
includes items dated 2016, together with some pre-2016 titles that were not included in earlier compilations.
