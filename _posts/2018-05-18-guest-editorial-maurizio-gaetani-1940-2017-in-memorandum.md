## Guest Editorial: Maurizio Gaetani 1940–2017, In Memorandum

Albertiana Guest Editorial![]({{ '/assets/albertiana/2018/05/44-5-Baud-Cover.jpg' | relative_url }})

B[aud, A. 2018. Maurizio Gaetani 1940-2017: In Memorandum. Albertiana vol. 44, p. 60-63.]({{
'/assets/albertiana/2018/05/44-5-Baud.pdf' | relative_url }})

A personal letter in memory of Maurizio Gaetani by Aymon Baud. Maurizio Gaetani passed away on 19 December, 2017 in
Milano, Italy. Gaetani was the former chairman of the STS and well-known Triassic (and Jurassic) stratigrapher
and paleontologist from Milano University. He will be dearly missed by the Triassic and Jurassic community.
