## Future Meeting: STRATI 2019

<div>

STRATI 2019

</div>

<div>

Meeting Date: Tuesday, July 2, 2019 to Friday, July 5, 2019

</div>

<div>

Location: Milano

</div>

<div>

Organisers: Prof. Marco Balini

</div>

<div>

Contact Email: [info.Strati2019@unimi.it](mailto:info.Strati2019@unimi.it)

</div>

<div>

Following a highly-successful 1st meeting held in Lisbon (Portugal) in 2013 and a 2nd held in Graz (Austria) in 2015, the
3rd International Congress on Stratigraphy will be held in Italy, from 2 to 5 July 2019. The congress venue is Milano, in
the historical building of the Università degli Studi di Milano, but the pre- and post- congress field trips will be
organized in the Alps, Appennines and on the Italian islands. STRATI 2019 is organized by the Commissione Italiana di
Stratigrafia, Servizio Geologico d’Italia, Società Geologica Italiana, Società Paleontologica Italiana, Associazione
Italiana per lo studio del Quaternario, and Università degli Studi di Milano.

</div>
